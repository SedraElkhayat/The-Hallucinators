# phase1_step4_idea_card (idea_sharpener)

## customer

Freelance graphic designers in Egypt and the wider MENA region who work with several clients at once

## problem

Freelance graphic designers in Egypt need a way to track client projects, invoices sent, and revision rounds.

## solution

A digital template pack (Notion and Google Sheets) that helps freelance graphic designers in Egypt track client projects, invoices sent, and revision rounds.

## value_proposition

A bilingual (Arabic and English) digital template pack built for local freelancing habits, with no subscriptions and no collection of client data.
