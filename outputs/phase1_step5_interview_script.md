# phase1_step5_interview_script (mom_test_designer)

## questions

```json
[
  {
    "text": "Tell me about the last time you had multiple clients and needed to track projects, invoices, and revisions. How did you manage it?",
    "why": "Reveals how they currently handle project tracking and whether they find existing methods inadequate."
  },
  {
    "text": "Have you ever used any tools or templates to track client projects, invoices, or revisions? If so, what did you like or dislike about them?",
    "why": "Reveals their past experiences with similar tools and identifies potential pain points."
  },
  {
    "text": "What have you paid for in the past to manage client projects, invoices, or revisions? How much did it cost?",
    "why": "Reveals their willingness to pay for solutions and the cost they are currently willing to accept."
  },
  {
    "text": "How do you currently handle invoicing clients for your work? Have you ever used any tools or templates for this?",
    "why": "Reveals their current invoicing practices and whether they are open to using a digital template pack."
  },
  {
    "text": "Have you ever used a bilingual tool or template for your work? If so, how did it help or hinder your workflow?",
    "why": "Reveals their familiarity with bilingual tools and whether they value such a feature."
  },
  {
    "text": "What do you do when you need to track revisions for a client project? Have you ever used a system or tool for this?",
    "why": "Reveals their current revision tracking methods and whether they find them effective."
  }
]
```

## rules

```json
[
  "Ask about specific past events and current behaviors, not opinions or future plans.",
  "Avoid pitching the solution or describing the idea in any question.",
  "Focus on what the customer has already tried and what they have paid for.",
  "Look for commitment in terms of time, money, or introductions.",
  "Treat compliments as noise and focus on actionable behaviors."
]
```

```json
[
  "Would you buy the template pack?",

  "Do you think the template pack is useful?",
  "Would you use the template pack for tracking projects?"
]
```
