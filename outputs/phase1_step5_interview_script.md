## rules

```json
[
  "Ask about specific past events and current behavior, not opinions or the future.",
  "Avoid pitching the idea or describing the solution in any question.",
  "Focus on what they have already tried and what they have paid for.",
  "Look for commitment: time, money, and introductions.",
  "Treat compliments as noise and focus on actionable insights."
]
```

```json

  "What would you do if...",
  "What do you prefer..."
```
