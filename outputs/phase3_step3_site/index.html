The template pack includes Notion and Google Sheets templates for tracking client projects, invoices sent, and revision rounds.

No, the product does not collect any client data. It is designed to help you manage your projects without storing sensitive information.
Are there any subscriptions involved?
No, there are no subscriptions. The template pack is a one-time purchase with no recurring fees.
What file formats are the templates available in?
The templates are available in Notion and Google Sheets formats for easy access and use.
