FAQ

What is included in the template pack?
The template pack includes Notion and Google Sheets templates for tracking client projects, invoices sent, and revision rounds.
Can I use the templates in Arabic and English?
Yes, the templates are bilingual and support both Arabic and English.
What file formats are the templates available in?
The templates are available in Notion and Google Sheets formats for easy use.
Does the product help with invoicing?
The templates help manage invoices sent and track revision rounds, but they do not generate invoices automatically.
Can I use the templates for multiple clients?
Yes, the templates are designed to help manage multiple clients efficiently.
