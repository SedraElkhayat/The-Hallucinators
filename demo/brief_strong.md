# Founder brief (demo, SAMPLE)

**Starting idea:** A digital template pack (Notion and Google Sheets) that helps freelance graphic designers in Egypt track client projects, invoices sent, and revision rounds.

**Founder skills:** 4 years as a freelance designer; comfortable with Notion, Google Sheets and Canva; no coding.
**Budget:** USD 100 total.
**Time:** 8 hours per week for 3 months.
**Target market:** Freelance designers in Egypt and the wider MENA region who work with several clients at once.
**Values and limits:** Bilingual (Arabic and English). No subscriptions that lock users in. Will not collect client data.
